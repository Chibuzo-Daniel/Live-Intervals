For Individuals

How It Works

Three simple steps to proactive safety

01
⏱️

Set Your Interval

Choose how often Live Intervals checks in on you - every 5, 15, or 30 minutes. Longer intervals mean better battery life. Shorter intervals mean faster response. You are in control.

02
📍

Automated Check-ins

Our system runs quietly in the background, verifying your location at each interval. No need to open the app or press a button. It just works, so you can focus on your journey.

03
🛡️

Offline Caching & Alerts

If a check-in is missed or your phone loses network connection, your last known locations are cached locally and sent the moment you are back online. Your trusted contacts get an alert with your location history.
